INDOOR SCENES

Use build_room for each room. It makes the floor, the ceiling and the walls. It makes gaps for doors and windows.

build_room(id, at, size [width, depth, height], doors, windows).
- at is the floor centre.
- width runs along x. depth runs along z.
- A door is {wall: "n"|"s"|"e"|"w", at, width}. at is the distance in metres along the wall, from its west end (n, s) or north end (e, w).
- Walls carry the wall tag. Floors carry floor. Ceilings carry ceiling.

Stairs. Use build_stairs(from, yaw, height). It returns a path. Give the path to move_along so a person climbs.

Lighting. Use set_lighting with look "indoor".

Cutaway. Call set_cutaway. The viewer then hides ceilings and the walls between the camera and its target. Place the camera high and outside the room. The cutaway is only for room walls and ceilings from build_room. Outdoors, move the camera instead.

A room for another place. When one chapter happens in a building away from the main scene (a hearing, a meeting), build the room far from the street, for example 200 m away and outside the OpenStreetMap footprints. Cut the camera to it (cut true). Use look "indoor" for that chapter.

Real buildings. A layout that no public source gives is an estimate. Set estimated true and source "estimated" on every object. Say so in the caption: "Layout is approximate".

Furniture. Use table, chair, banquet-table-8, stage, sofa, reception-desk, turnstile, lift-door, escalator, plant and notice-board. Keep 0.9 m or more for a walking path.
