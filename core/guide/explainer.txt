BUILDING AN EXPLAINER

Plan before you place. Write the chapter list first. A good explainer has 8 to 14 chapters of 10 to 15 s each. The total is 1.5 to 3 minutes.

Chapter structure.
1. Set the scene: place, time, normal life.
2. One chapter for each step of the event, in time order.
3. A closing chapter for what followed and what people learned.

For each chapter call add_chapter with title, clock, caption, focus, shot and source.
- title: 2 to 5 words.
- clock: the time of day when you know it, for example "21:21".
- caption: one to three short sentences. At most 280 characters. A 10 s chapter fits about 160 characters. Every character needs 0.06 s on screen.
- focus: the objects the viewer must see.
- source: the URL or document name for the claim.

Sources and estimates.
- Every measured fact needs a source. Mark it measured true and give source.
- Every distance, layout, count or time without a source is an estimate. Set estimated true. Start its source with "estimated".
- Never invent a source. Say "estimated" when you do not know.
- Say in a caption when a layout is approximate.

Place. Each chapter happens at its real place. An event at another place gets its own set there, and the camera cuts to it (set_camera with cut true). For example, a committee that met in a building gets a simple room (build_room) away from the street, not a sign in the street.
- Put a sign or a callout only where and when its fact is true. A sign that a chapter lists in focus shows its label only in the chapters that list it. A sign in no chapter focus (a street name) shows in every chapter. A callout must lie inside one chapter (validate, code callout_outside_chapter).

Shot variety. Open each chapter with a wide or medium shot. Use a close shot for a person or a detail. Use top for a layout. At least 3 shot types in a story of 12 chapters or more.

Text. Use plain words and short sentences. Contrast between text and its background is at least 4.5 to 1.

Real events. Use generic faces. Put no names on figures. Do not name victims. Use cautious words for contested facts.

Quality bars checked by validate and by the eval.
- 10 or more people variants and 8 poses. A walk cycle.
- A shot in every chapter. No hidden focus object. No blocked view: nothing stands between the camera and the subject. Clear a view only by moving the camera.
- No collision, no figure off the ground, no vehicle in the wrong lane (guide "motion").
- Uniformed forces wear the right outfit or sourced uniform colours (guide "people").
- Every object has a source or the estimated flag.
- Captions are short enough and stay long enough on screen.
- Captions are also written to an .srt file by export_scene.
