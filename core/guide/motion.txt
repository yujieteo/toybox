MOTION

Things in the scene are solid. Plan every path so that nothing passes into anything else. validate samples the whole scene every 0.25 s and reports each problem with its time span.
- collision: two objects pass into each other. People, vehicles, props, furniture, walls and OpenStreetMap buildings all count. People count too: two people must not stand in one place.
- off_ground: a person or a vehicle floats above or sinks into the ground. Put it on the ground, a floor, a stage or stairs.
- wrong_lane: a moving vehicle drives on the wrong side of the road.
move_along answers with "collisions" when its new path runs into something. Fix it at once.

Vehicles.
- Traffic keeps left in Singapore. On a road along z, a vehicle that goes north (to -z) keeps to the west lane (x below the road centre). A vehicle that goes south (to +z) keeps to the east lane. set_backdrop drive "right" changes the side.
- One vehicle in a lane at a time, or keep a gap. A car is about 4.6 m long and a bus 10 to 12 m. Two vehicles in one lane need the same speed and a gap of at least 2 m, or the faster one goes first.
- A vehicle that waits off screen waits outside the paths of the others, for example beyond the end of the street. A vehicle that leaves drives on to a point off screen. It never disappears.
- A vehicle never drives through a person. Time the crossing so that the person is on the pavement when the vehicle passes.
- A wreck that takes the place of a vehicle (a car that burns) appears at the same place and time as the vehicle goes (scale swap). Nothing else may vanish.

People.
- A person keeps about 0.5 m from the next person. A crowd needs a space for each person. Give the people in a crowd different end points.
- People who walk the same line go the same way at the same speed, one after the other. People who walk in opposite directions use lines at least 0.6 m apart.
- Plan crowds so that no path crosses a person who already stands still. For example, the first person to arrive takes the place furthest along the path.
- A person in a car does not show while the car drives. The person appears beside the door when the car stops (animate scale from 0.001 to 1 at that time), then walks away from the car. Never walk a person through the side of a vehicle.
- Leave a free path for people who must walk through a crowd (paramedics, police).
- Walk round street furniture: lamp posts, trees, bins, benches and bus stops.

Held gear. A shield or a baton follows the hand. A person who carries a long shield walks (3 m/s or slower). A run swings the shield flat.
